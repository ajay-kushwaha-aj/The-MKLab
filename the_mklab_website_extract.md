### Heading & Intro
- **H1:** `Research`
- **Intro:** `The ongoing research work can be organized around four interconnected themes:`

### Theme 1: Basic Biology of Long Non-Coding RNAs
- **Image URL:** `https://sites.google.com/sitesv-images-rt/APXyLQd1wnGdTHXqm08U1uTlg-hcjbYpWpSZfStqH7jD7ZpXGYHqOXUqOhCw1w05kXYKc0n6ER_j87iRFE0aoOagjzWAH-g1aNjNpvA5WdekCIdyXqqMufdQFOaxrULDpReuy5rTBN3BbOhTh0CsWwlRe66YtBhO3rbDJcTSyOZ6kRGLvfIztq9xE8M62klg3HK7CPoQX98ZuefozW8KAJf13oLD2d9aPhMTqGfdcCXYhFQ=w1280`
- **Description:**
  > We have reported how inefficient splicing contributes to the transcriptional complexity of lncRNAs (Basu *et al*, RNA Biology, 2023). Our research investigates whether lncRNAs can encode microproteins and identifies the splicing events and RNA modifications enriched in such cases.

### Theme 2: Gene Regulation by Non-Coding RNAs
- **Image URL:** `https://sites.google.com/sitesv-images-rt/APXyLQdzg3438zGFYBAk-FErVFQfDHGyfHowjBynyUE-Op2AE2w0a5KmAcr-MhIQZnhxcdE2PNruMY-ChcJP7vj7sAyomRwP5BMk3eheKgK143g70uc0BGgDOTEaIcZFpsvCxl3HBL_V5m92YIFEms6JhQyOwCRyQwKgSZ8Ef5FuNjJaajUC6pY80-qRqhwrRVsMv70w72ye3d-C5vt1gLnF7r6P7X2whBA-J8lfLw=w1280`
- **Description:**
  > We study how different classes of ncRNAs regulate gene expression. This includes the intriguing spatial pairing of lncRNAs with nearby protein-coding genes (PCGs) and how proximity may drive transcriptional activity. We also investigate enhancer-transcribed RNAs (eRNAs) for their roles in transcriptional condensate formation and super enhancer-derived lncRNAs (SE-lncRNAs) for global gene expression reprogramming in disease contexts.

### Theme 3: Epitranscriptomics in RNA Function
- **Image URL:** `https://sites.google.com/sitesv-images-rt/APXyLQd8T9JUj28QSQXMY_9rImTvOAA9ZlZ1m0MpUyG7hNhcDJK7rjh6gT2ljw484vDRsEEukS1sWL4Da1agpKOkqrkYkqASc-erA7kU7xspC8ZjhXSb-EGm_f3LHuqBgkg5sJQBqoxxQKxxlNVA0nmzqWdSHLOgOkCy056TSkpLX7-wyVS7kvGXgIAYyXMruT6xWcqRqe9LeFdDfKqlCe2kGrmf3yIPIaJ6msX3ZPI1LPM=w1280`
- **Description:**
  > Our lab delves into the role of RNA modifications in regulating ncRNA function, particularly in eRNA-mediated condensate formation and lncRNA translation into microproteins. We are actively working to identify unknown RNA modifiers, especially erasers for reversible modifications those may have critical regulatory roles in disease.

### Theme 4: Tool and Database Development
- **Image URL:** `https://sites.google.com/sitesv-images-rt/APXyLQd_H6rV-WdEPp0KCfKJY3yCH29dQHHpVgyPhnqSKAcVm-PSTezrTA06vkXIgz-4d3l5wRtd-XL1E_cbEd6Y26-m8IgFrfY5G7mYSg3VkPTTgaJhuIK4VDQV0P7wXxqxvdTb71GA9AACsxh_pWYza_XWx1J6joZCUH_DE4g0BT9q5j3uPNWckU-gNUFRssaG7O0BvSkoC417v8aLu8NSDClQxihG33EDqCONCw=w1280`
- **Description:**
  > As a computational lab, we create innovative tools and machine-learning frameworks to facilitate biological discovery:
- **Bullets:**
  - `MAGICAL – Predicts genetic interactions in cancer (Dey et al, 2024 PLOS Comp Bio)`
  - `MAGICALDB – hosts known and predicted genetic interactions`
  - `Utilizing single-cell RNASeq to identify tumorigenic cell types`
  - `Development of prognostic models, e.g., UVA8 for gliomas (Kiran et al, 2018, Molecular Neurobiology) and DAS for gynecological cancers. (Mavika et al, 2025, International Journal Clinical Oncology)`
  - `CATCH – Calculates transcript complexity of a gene (Basu et al, 2024, MethodX)`
  - `PROMISE – Performs survival analysis for a combination of genes`
